Our philosophy

Less markup.
More meaning.

We believe the best things shouldn’t require a logo to be understood. SILLAGE makes considered alternatives to the icons you already love — composed with uncommon materials and an editor’s eye.

Our in-house perfumers build original formulas around the accords you know, then take them somewhere new.

02

Materials over margin.

Japanese quartz movements, sapphire-coated crystal, French fragrance oils. The money goes into the object.

03

Fewer, better things.

A tight, deliberate collection. No seasonal noise. Pieces designed to be worn daily and kept for years.
